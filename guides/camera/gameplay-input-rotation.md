# Gameplay camera input and continuous rotation

Reference: the original PC 1.67 `TS3W.exe` identified in the
[native reference](../../evidence/native/README.md). Names and field meanings
below are analyst assignments. The annotations are retained in the actual
Ghidra pseudocode; original instructions govern SIMD propagation and x87 ABI.

## Physical middle-mouse and capture

The [original vtable and trigger excerpts](../../evidence/native/gameplay-camera-input-data.json) identify normal-camera resources `F0FF5598:00000000:2148660442B22211` and `F0FF5598:00000000:62EC9813675C4070` in original `GameBin/UI/UI.package`. Both bind `?shift mouse_button_middle` to `CameraFlagRotate` (`0x02541005`) and continuous mouse movement to `CameraMove` (`0x0254000E`). These are selected original-data excerpts, not a complete trigger-parser proof.

The [gameplay constructor](../../evidence/native/functions/00972450_MainCamera_Construct.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00972450_MainCamera_Construct.c)) installs primary vtable `0x01046D00`. Its `+0x40` cell contains `0x0096FF10`: the [held-motion setter](../../evidence/native/functions/0096FF10_MainCamera_SetScriptMotionFlags.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/0096FF10_MainCamera_SetScriptMotionFlags.c)) sets/clears flags at controller `+0x880`. Its `+0x44` cell contains `0x0096FF40`: the [rotation setter](../../evidence/native/functions/0096FF40_MainCamera_SetRotatingAndCapture.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/0096FF40_MainCamera_SetRotatingAndCapture.c)) gates input rotation and captures/releases the pointer. The [script motion wrapper](../../evidence/native/functions/0073D6F0_ScriptCore_CameraController_Camera_SetMotion.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/0073D6F0_ScriptCore_CameraController_Camera_SetMotion.c)) and [script rotation wrapper](../../evidence/native/functions/0073D730_ScriptCore_CameraController_Camera_SetRotating.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/0073D730_ScriptCore_CameraController_Camera_SetRotating.c)) dispatch to these slots; physical MMB is established by the trigger path below.

The [input callback](../../evidence/native/functions/00962210_CameraController_ForwardTriggerCallback.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00962210_CameraController_ForwardTriggerCallback.c)) forwards the message and record to owner virtual `+0x5C`, whose gameplay target is the [trigger handler](../../evidence/native/functions/009747F0_MainCamera_HandleInputTrigger.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/009747F0_MainCamera_HandleInputTrigger.c)). The handler runs when enabled at controller `+0x14`. For rotation trigger `0x02541005`, message kind `0x5ECE0000` enables rotation; the release branch disables it. Both invoke the [input rotation gate](../../evidence/native/functions/00962680_CameraInput_SetRotationDrag.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00962680_CameraInput_SetRotationDrag.c)), copying current sample `+0x500` into anchor `+0x4F0` and previous `+0x510`, while changing only mask bit `2`. They do not reset stored yaw or retained angular velocity.

Capture calls the [service wrapper](../../evidence/native/functions/00599950_InputService_SetMouseCapture.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00599950_InputService_SetMouseCapture.c)) with `(true,true)` on press and `(false,false)` on release. The [capture implementation](../../evidence/native/functions/00599680_InputService_UpdateMouseCapture.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00599680_InputService_UpdateMouseCapture.c)) keeps the press position as anchor and hides the cursor. This mode recenters at that point, rather than the window center. Its restore flag `+0x61` is the inverse of the second argument, verified at `0x005998F1/0x00599907`; the annotated prototype preserves this distinction. MMB release shows the cursor and ends capture without restoring a separate pre-center position.

The [input service dispatcher](../../evidence/native/functions/00599380_InputService_DispatchMouseEvent.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00599380_InputService_DispatchMouseEvent.c)) discards the synthetic movement exactly at the stored integer anchor. It dispatches real movement and warps back via window virtual `+0x84`. For captured camera movement, the trigger handler reads the [capture anchor](../../evidence/native/functions/00599970_InputService_GetCapturedMouseAnchor.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00599970_InputService_GetCapturedMouseAnchor.c)), queues `anchor - eventPosition` through the [warp-compensation accumulator](../../evidence/native/functions/009625B0_CameraInput_QueueWarpCompensation.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/009625B0_CameraInput_QueueWarpCompensation.c)), and installs current position as anchor through the [sample setter](../../evidence/native/functions/00962570_CameraInput_SetCurrentMouse.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00962570_CameraInput_SetCurrentMouse.c)). The collector subsequently adjusts previous and anchor by this compensation, preserving actual movement and excluding the recenter discontinuity.

The [forward coordinate conversion](../../evidence/native/functions/00599270_InputService_PixelsToLogical.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00599270_InputService_PixelsToLogical.c)) scales client coordinates into renderer logical dimensions. The [reverse conversion](../../evidence/native/functions/00599300_InputService_LogicalToPixels.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00599300_InputService_LogicalToPixels.c)) converts back for integer cursor placement. Therefore a constructor gain is not automatically radians per physical display pixel.

The [settings reader](../../evidence/native/functions/00969F60_CameraManager_LoadRotationPreferences.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00969F60_CameraManager_LoadRotationPreferences.c)) reads horizontal/vertical rotation inversion with default false. The [controller inversion update](../../evidence/native/functions/009704E0_MainCamera_ApplyRotationInversion.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/009704E0_MainCamera_ApplyRotationInversion.c)) sets each bank through the [sign setter](../../evidence/native/functions/00961EE0_CameraInputBank_SetAxisInverted.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00961EE0_CameraInputBank_SetAxisInverted.c)): false gives `+1`, true `-1`. Angular yaw gain is bank `+0x14` multiplied by bank `+0x30`; gains come through the [bank tuning copy](../../evidence/native/functions/00962400_CameraInput_ApplyGainTables.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00962400_CameraInput_ApplyGainTables.c)) and the 95-word gameplay tuning setter. The constructor's positive fallback gains do not establish the values supplied by a loaded tuning profile.

## Input aggregation

The [input collector](../../evidence/native/functions/00962C90_CameraInput_CollectRateAndImpulse.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00962C90_CameraInput_CollectRateAndImpulse.c)) returns two nine-float blocks: continuous rates first, impulses second. Each contains XYZ translation at byte offset `0x00`, angular pitch/yaw at `0x10`, and zoom at `0x20`. Its [bank accumulator](../../evidence/native/functions/009622C0_CameraInput_AccumulateBankOutputs.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/009622C0_CameraInput_AccumulateBankOutputs.c)) combines four banks with stride `0x100`.

Rotation uses input mask bit `2`: the bank-one impulse vector at input
`+0x1E0` receives pitch from negative screen Y displacement and yaw from X
displacement. The selected bank gains are applied by the collector. The
radius-eight deadzone and mask bit `4` concern panning, not MMB yaw.

The [drag-state accessor](../../evidence/native/functions/00961F50_CameraInput_IsPanDragActive.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00961F50_CameraInput_IsPanDragActive.c)) tests panning bit `4`; the frame uses it to suppress edge scrolling. The [scalar accessor](../../evidence/native/functions/00961F30_CameraInput_GetBankScalar.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00961F30_CameraInput_GetBankScalar.c)) reads a selected bank/scalar. Frame call `0x0097688B` selects bank one scalar two or three for negative/positive zoom speed. It supplies no yaw limit.

The [horizontal basis transform](../../evidence/native/functions/0097D940_CameraInput_TranslationToHorizontalWorld.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/0097D940_CameraInput_TranslationToHorizontalWorld.c)) transforms translation into world space using independently normalized horizontal right and negative-forward camera basis vectors, preserving Y. It does not normalize yaw.

## Frame integration and publication

The [complete camera frame](../../evidence/native/functions/009758D0_GameplayCamera_UpdateNativeFrame.asm) ([annotated pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/009758D0_GameplayCamera_UpdateNativeFrame.c)) drains input at `0x00975CEB`. At `0x00975D56..0x00975D71`, it forms angular speed as `-(continuousRate + impulse/deltaSeconds)`. The sign scalar at `0x00FAB218` is binary32 `-1`, bytes `00 00 80 BF`; this multiplication applies to the angular output. Translation preserves the positive sum.

Pitch/yaw velocity occupies controller `+0x7F0/+0x7F4`, while continuous
pitch/yaw occupies `+0x7D0/+0x7D4`. Fresh input replaces retained velocity.
Without fresh angular input, the frame tests the two-axis velocity magnitude
against binary32 `0.1` at `0x0104487C` (`CD CC CC 3D`). Above that threshold,
it multiplies retained velocity by `pow(rotationRetention, deltaSeconds)`.
Otherwise it replaces velocity with the fresh, normally zero input.

The [power import](../../evidence/native/functions/00F7F7D6_MSVCR80_DLL__CIpow.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00F7F7D6_MSVCR80_DLL__CIpow.c)) is the `MSVCR80.DLL::_CIpow` thunk, not an independently recovered power implementation. Its x87 operands are visible at the caller: retention `+0x8D4` is loaded first, then frame seconds, before call `0x00976815`. The [tuning setter](../../evidence/native/functions/00970740_GameplayCamera_ApplyNativeTuning.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00970740_GameplayCamera_ApplyNativeTuning.c)) accepts 95 words and supplies that retention from word 41, after the 40 input-bank words. This establishes data ownership, not a guessed default.

The ordinary rotation branch `0x00977EBC..0x00977ED5` adds
`retainedVelocity * deltaSeconds` directly to the angles. It does not track
a separate yaw destination, select a shortest angular distance, wrap yaw, or
apply a yaw clamp.

The [zoom-bound damping helper](../../evidence/native/functions/009700D0_GameplayCamera_AdjustZoomRateNearBounds.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/009700D0_GameplayCamera_AdjustZoomRateNearBounds.c)) operates on zoom and its cached speed/direction fields. It is not the yaw damping helper. The [zoom-dependent pitch limits](../../evidence/native/functions/00970240_MainCamera_EvaluatePitchLimitsAtZoom.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00970240_MainCamera_EvaluatePitchLimitsAtZoom.c)) supply pitch limits; `0x00977F80..0x00978028` replaces pitch lane zero and preserves yaw lane one.

The [direction reconstruction](../../evidence/native/functions/0097D620_GameplayCamera_DirectionFromPitchYaw.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/0097D620_GameplayCamera_DirectionFromPitchYaw.c)) evaluates `(sin(yaw)*cos(pitch), sin(pitch), -cos(yaw)*cos(pitch))` without rewriting the stored angles. Its output pointer is preserved in EAX at return; SIMD lane W repeats X.

The [pose commit](../../evidence/native/functions/00974DE0_GameplayCamera_CommitFramePose.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00974DE0_GameplayCamera_CommitFramePose.c)) publishes the prepared pose to the camera object. Its sky/base transition interpolates pitch; ordinary mouse yaw remains the continuous stored value. The [yaw getter](../../evidence/native/functions/00970EF0_GameplayCamera_GetYawDegrees.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/00970EF0_GameplayCamera_GetYawDegrees.c)) returns `yaw * radiansToDegrees` without wrapping. The [public getter](../../evidence/native/functions/0073D8D0_ScriptCore_CameraController_Camera_GetYawDegrees.asm) ([pseudocode](../../evidence/native/functions/0073D8D0_ScriptCore_CameraController_Camera_GetYawDegrees.c)) dispatches to it at `0x0073D903`.
