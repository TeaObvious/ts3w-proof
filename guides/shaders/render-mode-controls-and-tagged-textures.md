The reference executable is identified centrally in the [proof reference](../../references/retained-proof-overview.md); pointer/flag names below describe observed offsets, not recovered C++ types. The genuine Ghidra C retains its existing ABI uncertainty and embedded instruction-backed annotations. No gameplay or GPU output was executed to establish these facts.

## Inputs, producers and consumers

The dispatcher calls the below-height predicate and mode-control routine directly; their exact sites are in the [direct-call index](../../evidence/native/direct-calls.tsv). That index also records all51 direct E8 calls in these complete bodies. A false `target_has_pseudocode` means the destination remains a reference leaf without a canonical paired body, not that the call did not occur in the instructions. Calls are static relationships, never executed counts. Existing incoming references to newly paired targets remain linked.

The mode-control routine reads its receiver/camera/surface/provider values at their actual sites, publishes pointers to control vectors through the existing parameter registry, invokes the selected technique and restores its temporary state. The tagged-slot routine publishes registry payloads conditionally and then dispatches a draw; a skipped payload update is distinct from actively writing null for an admitted invalid/tagged slot. These bodies do not establish who last produced all receiver fields, each control's complete shader meaning, or the lifetime of registry payloads beyond the selected consumer path.

## Types, state and limits

The below-height predicate consumes ECX and returns EAX with plainRET. The mode-control routine consumes ECX plus one stackDWORD and returns withRET4. The tagged-slot routine consumes ECX plus four stackDWORDs andRET16. The selector's existing fastcall/decompiler register placeholders must not be rewritten as a proved three-parameter C signature merely because three stackslots are read. No parameter/local/type/storage or original bytes were changed.

The five canonical pairs cover673 instructions and2416 contiguous owned bytes:209,1712,101,153 and241 respectively. Their bounds contain no unowned alignment bytes. The preserved decompiler warnings and incomplete callee prototype are part of the evidence. Full provider ownership, higher-level flag meanings, texture-map identities, all lower shader/technique bodies, exceptional device behavior and runtime/pixel equivalence remain open.
