## Scalar admission and Z coefficients

Apply53D4E0 uses original x87 comparisons of absolute X/Y/Z with Single epsilon bits34000000. Components ordered greater than epsilon count as active; unordered comparisons take the inactive route. Zero active axes skip dispatch; one selects its X/Y/Z branch; multiple select the arbitrary-axis branch. Sole-axis sign and magnitude are not used to scale or reverse the chosen branch. The angle is compared to the stored zero word with UCOMISS/LAHF/TEST; positive and negative zero skip, while the unordered comparison admits dispatch. This is the physical branch policy, not a general finite-number validation rule.

The selected branch performs one MULSS with the Single degree factor bits3C8EFA35 before passing radians. Z helper512DC0 uses x87 FSIN and FCOS, stores Single sine/cosine, and appends the resulting matrix. For an identity receiver the relevant row-vector coefficients are [c,-s;s,c]: X'=X*c+Y*s and Y'=Y*c-X*s. The helper updates the full existing transform and raises its classification word at+40 to at least2; it does not replace prior local effects by a new standalone rotation. X/Y/arbitrary helper algorithms remain outside the selected closure. Their call selection is established here, not complete3D output equivalence.

Original x87 extended intermediates, transcendental instructions, Single stores and scalar/vector multiplication order govern. An algebraically similar portable trig/FMA expression is not evidence of identical bits. Infinity, NaN propagation, singular inverse policy and complete3D numerics are not promoted to a broader reconstructed contract.
